The full CC BY 4.0 legal text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Why CC BY 4.0

Credit is the only condition, which agencies and nonprofits accept readily. It is compatible with
upstream CC BY data such as the USDA farmers market directory. Version 4.0 covers database rights.

## What the license covers

Only what this project contributes: the compiled, structured facts. Third-party content keeps its
own terms, which is why the Food Web publishes facts and links to originals, not descriptive text
or photos copied from other sites.

## Contributor statement

By contributing, you confirm the facts are yours to share and agree they are released under
CC BY 4.0, with credit to you as a named source.

## Source terms

A first review found no stated data license on most upstream sources. Status is tracked in
[docs/CHARTER.md](docs/CHARTER.md) under "Not yet". Nothing is published from a source until its
terms are understood or permission is given.
