# Colorado Open Food Web: a summary for regional food projects

**An open, shared, community-maintained dataset of how Colorado's food connects: the producers, markets, and the
restaurants and stores that carry their food.** Free to use, open to inspect, and kept accurate by the people who
know their area best.

## The problem

Information about local food is scattered across state programs, federal directories, market associations, county
nonprofits, and individual websites. Each holds a piece, none agrees with the others, and each goes stale on its own
schedule. Every project that wants to help people find local food ends up rebuilding the same list.

## What exists today

- **1,518 listings across Colorado** in the first public release (v2026.10.1): farms and ranches, farmers markets,
  food makers, restaurants, grocers and farm stores, drawn from USDA's Local Food Directories, Colorado Proud, the
  Colorado Farmers Market Association, and hand research, with the source of every field recorded.
- **Sources refreshed on a schedule**, with every change reviewed before it is applied.
- **Apps already built on it:** the [Colorado Farm Trail](https://coloradofarmtrail.com) map, a CSA finder, and a
  local food guide. The first regional slice (Chaffee County) was built with Guidestone Colorado.
- **Everything is public:** the data, the code, and the schema, at
  [github.com/open-food-web/colorado-open-food-web](https://github.com/open-food-web/colorado-open-food-web).

## What a partner gets

1. **Your listings, cleaned and linked.** Duplicates merged, links checked, each record tied to its sources, in a
   standard format you can reuse on your own site.
2. **A gaps report for your area.** Which producers and markets we have that you don't, and the reverse; what is stale
   or missing.
3. **Your corrections stick.** A correction from you is applied on every update, so the next refresh never undoes
   it, and the record keeps its sources.
4. **Free tools.** A statewide map, a CSA finder and a local food guide you can link to, and data your own projects
   can read directly.
5. **A place in the statewide picture.** Your region's producers become visible to everyone using the data.

## What we ask

- Share your listings (a spreadsheet or a link is fine) and tell us what is wrong or missing in your area.
- Agree that the facts you contribute are released under [CC BY 4.0](../LICENSE), credited to you by name.
- If you can, tell your producers about it and point your site at the data.

## How it stays trustworthy

- **Facts only.** No copied descriptions or photos. Records link to their sources instead.
- **Contact details are checked.** An email appears only if the business lists it on its own website or Facebook page.
- **Producers are in control.** Anyone can ask for a correction or removal and it is honored.
- **Open and credited.** Open license, open process, and every field shows where it came from.
- **Neutral.** It does not rank, promote or endorse any producer.

## How to start

1. [Open an issue](https://github.com/open-food-web/colorado-open-food-web/issues/new/choose) with a correction, a
   missing listing, or "we'd like to partner".
2. Send your list. We return a cleaned copy and your gaps report.
3. Decide together how corrections reach us, and whether your site should read the data.
