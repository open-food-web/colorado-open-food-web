About the Colorado Open Food Web

The Colorado Open Food Web is an open source data project. It keeps an accurate, up-to-date listing of Colorado's food producers (farms, ranches, orchards, food makers and wineries) and of their sales channels: where and how you can buy from them, such as farm stands, farmers markets, CSAs, pickup points, delivery, online orders, and the shops and grocers that carry their food.

Everything here is sourced from publicly available web sources. Nothing is guessed: a detail appears only if a source states it, and every record keeps a pointer to where it came from.

How it works

  • Public sources, combined. Listings are pulled on a schedule from public directories (such as Colorado Proud, the Colorado Farmers Market Association and the USDA Local Food Portal) and from each business's own website and public social pages. Records from different sources for the same business are merged into one listing.
  • Kept current. Sources are re-checked regularly. Every refresh is a reviewed change that shows exactly what is new, changed or gone, so the listing stays accurate over time rather than going stale.
  • Reviewed by people. Nothing is applied until a person approves it. Details a reviewer or the business has confirmed are never overwritten by an automatic check.
  • Privacy first. An email address appears only if the business publishes it itself. Where a business asked us not to share its street address, the map shows only the town.
  • Open and versioned. The data is free to use with credit under CC BY 4.0 and published in numbered releases, so other projects can build on the same list. The code and the full history are in the GitHub repository.

Many listings are not on the map

Most Colorado producers sell by CSA, online, at markets or from a home that is not open to visitors. A listing only gets a map pin when a source confirms a place where people can buy or visit, so a sparse map means missing confirmed places, not missing farms. Counties differ a lot in how much is mapped so far. The directory lists everything, mapped or not. The supply network shows how producers, shops and restaurants connect.

    Fix something or add a listing

    Spot a mistake, a closed business or a missing producer? Every listing has Suggest an update and Remove this listing buttons, and you can add a listing. Reports are public, reviewed by a person and credited to you. Producers can ask to correct or remove their own record, and removal requests are honored first.
