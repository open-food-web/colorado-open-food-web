A market changes its hours. The Food Web notices.

Below is a made-up farmers market with a website. Change its hours, season or location, or use a preset. A watcher re-reads the site every few seconds, spots what changed, and queues it for a person to approve before the listing changes.

    How it works. The "website" is a real web page served from your browser, and the watcher fetches and reads it the way the pipeline reads a real site: it parses the hours and dates from the page text and compares them with the last reading. Nothing leaves your browser and nothing is added to the Food Web. In the real pipeline, detected changes arrive as a pull request, and a person merging it is the approval. A date or time the page doesn't state is never guessed.
