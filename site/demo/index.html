Prototype

From a website to a listing, and notice when it changes.

Paste a farm, ranch or maker's web address. The demo reads the public site and fills in a Food Web style listing: contact details, hours, how to buy, and what they sell. Check the same site again later and it shows what changed.

Try:

How it works. Facts only: names, phone, address, hours, links, and a short pointer to the page that mentions each way to buy. Nothing is copied from the site, and results are marked unverified until a person checks them. A field that disappears is flagged for review, never deleted. Pages are read through a free public proxy because browsers can't read other sites directly; the proxy checks the site's robots.txt and identifies itself as ColoradoOpenFoodWeb. Snapshots stay in this browser only. This is a demo, separate from the dataset: nothing here is added to the Food Web.
