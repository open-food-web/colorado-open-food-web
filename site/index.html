Who grows, sells and serves Colorado's food, and how they connect.

The Colorado Open Food Web is a free, shared dataset of Colorado's producers, markets, and the restaurants and stores that carry their food. It is built from public sources, checked, and kept accurate in the open, so every project that helps people find local food can start from the same list.

    How it stays accurate

    1. Sources are pulled on a schedule. USDA's Local Food Directories, Colorado Proud, the Colorado Farmers Market Association, and hand research. Every field records where it came from.
    2. Every change is reviewed. A refresh opens a pull request that shows exactly what is new, changed or gone. Nothing is applied until a person approves it.
    3. Contact details are checked. An email appears only if the business lists it on its own website or Facebook page.
    4. Releases are versioned. Apps pin a release and check its checksum, so an update never surprises them.

    Facts only: no copied descriptions or photos. Records link to their sources instead.

      See something wrong? Fix it.

      Every business in the web has a Flag a change button. It opens a short form on GitHub, already filled in with the business's name. A free GitHub account is the only sign-in; your report is public, reviewed by a person, and credited to you. Producers can also ask to correct or remove their own record.
